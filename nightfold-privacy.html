Nightfold Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Nightfold is a free app by Vibe Coded Studio that plays an animation when you open or close a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold. This policy explains what the app does with information on your phone. Short version: nothing leaves your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Nightfold has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and it does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device.

What stays on your phone

  • Home-screen pictures. When you tap "Capture inner screen" or "Capture outer screen" (or after you unlock, as a backup), Nightfold takes a screenshot of your home screen so the effect can show your real wallpaper and icons. These pictures are saved in Nightfold's own app folder on your phone (Android/data/com.vibecodedstudio.nightfold), which other apps can't open. They are never shared, and Android deletes them when you uninstall the app.
  • Hinge and screen state. While the effect is on, Nightfold watches the fold angle and which screen is active, only to know when to play the animation. This is not stored or shared.
  • Your settings (effect choice, speeds, your opening timing) are saved on the phone.

Shizuku

Nightfold needs Shizuku, a separate free app you install and start yourself. Nightfold uses it only to show the effect over both screens, to take the home-screen pictures above, and to bring its own window back after a capture. Nightfold never uses it to read your messages, files, accounts or other apps' data.

Children

Nightfold does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
